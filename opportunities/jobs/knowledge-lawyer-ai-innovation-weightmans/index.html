About the role

Weightmans LLP is a top-50 UK law firm. This Knowledge Lawyer role sits in the firm's Product & Innovation team and focuses on artificial intelligence and innovation in how legal services are delivered. It is offered across the firm's Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool and Manchester offices on a hybrid basis.

This is a rolling vacancy with no fixed closing date, and the salary is not disclosed in the listing.
