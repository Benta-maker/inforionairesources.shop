About

Connecting Knowledge, Innovation and Opportunity

Who We Are

InfoOnAIResources is a global AI intelligence and opportunity platform connecting innovation ecosystems worldwide, with particular attention to Africa, South Asia and the Middle East and North Africa.

We make technological developments accessible by connecting information with educational opportunities, professional resources, research, practical applications and international collaboration.

We recognise that innovation is not confined to established technology centres. Knowledge, expertise and technological solutions emerge from diverse geographical regions and professional communities.

Our purpose is to connect these contributions, facilitate the exchange of knowledge and make meaningful participation in technological advancement more accessible.

Our Vision

A globally connected innovation ecosystem in which knowledge, expertise and opportunities move across geographical boundaries, enabling individuals and organisations to participate in and contribute to technological advancement.

Our Mission

We connect global innovation ecosystems, transforming the exchange of knowledge, technology and expertise into opportunities, partnerships and actionable intelligence for professionals, students and entrepreneurs across Africa, South Asia, MENA and beyond.

Innovation flows in every direction. Opportunity should too.

What you will find here

  • The Weekly Intelligence Brief — every Monday, free. Subscribe on the homepage.
  • The Opportunity Database — AI jobs, scholarships and fellowships, programmes and grants. Every listing links directly to the source organisation's own application page; you always apply on their site, never here.
  • Deep Dives — long-form analysis translating major AI developments into industry-specific implications.

Who runs InfoOnAIResources

InfoOnAIResources is operated by Iman Holdings Ltd, a company registered in Kenya, and is based in Nairobi, Kenya.
