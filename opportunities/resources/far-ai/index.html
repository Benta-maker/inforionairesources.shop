What FAR.AI is

FAR.AI is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit AI-safety research organisation based in Berkeley, California, with roughly 40+ staff and more than $40M in funding. It works on technical research and field-building aimed at ensuring advanced AI systems are safe and beneficial.

Why it matters for the Global South

FAR.AI hires in two ways: in person in Berkeley with visa sponsorship, and remotely in most countries through an employer of record. Remote roles are genuinely global, requiring roughly 3–5 hours of daily overlap with US Pacific time. That makes FAR.AI one of the few well-funded AI-safety employers whose roles are realistically open to candidates based in Africa, South Asia and MENA — the relevance hook for any FAR.AI role we list.
